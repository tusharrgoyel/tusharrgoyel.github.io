About Me

Hello! I'm Tushar Goyal

You can call me Tusharr Goyel

📍 Detroit, Michigan, USA

My work begins where the fuel meets the spark. I explore how flames ignite, evolve, and erupt, decoding the fundamental physics of autoignition, flame dynamics, and detonation dynamics. My focus spans from traditional hydrocarbons to the next generation of zero- and net-zero fuels, providing the insights needed to engineer cleaner and more powerful combustion systems.

To see what happens inside a flame, I use a suite of advanced tools. Optical diagnostics, spectroscopy, and chromatography are my eyes on the molecular level, with MWIR chemiluminescence and spectral calibration revealing the chemical story as it unfolds. When the physics hides from direct observation, I turn to numerical simulations (zero- to three-dimensional, RANS, LES, DNS), creating high-fidelity models of turbulent, lean premixed flames that illuminate what experiments cannot. Where simulation and reality diverge, I build the analytical bridges to connect them. And when the data becomes overwhelming, I distill it down to the essential physics, often using machine learning to build predictive surrogate models and accelerate design optimization.

This fundamental understanding is the engine that powers my practical work. I apply my insights into turbulence-chemistry interactions to solve critical challenges in: Gas Turbine Combustors: Ensuring stable performance and mitigating off-normal operations like lean blowout, high-altitude relight, and destructive combustion dynamics. Sustainable Aviation Fuels: Evaluating their real-world, end-use behavior to accelerate their adoption in aviation. Decarbonized Power Generation: Enabling the transition to zero- and low-carbon fuels by mastering their unique combustion characteristics. Microturbines: Advancing the efficiency and reliability of systems for combined heat and power applications. I also write my own code for numerical simulations, image processing, and machine learning, creating a seamless workflow to extract, validate, and accelerate our understanding of the underlying physics. My approach is built on a foundation of first principles and rigorous proof. I measure with precision. I simulate with fidelity, utilizing DNS/LES, CHT, and RANS methods. I benchmark my findings against established laminar baselines, including unstretched/stretched flames and perfectly stirred reactors. The ultimate goal is always to translate complex physics into actionable decisions that make combustion cleaner, more stable, and more efficient. What I Enjoy Translating Light into Logic: Turning raw spectra and images into concrete mechanisms and numbers—identifying chemical speciation via chemiluminescence and revealing flame structures through optical diagnostics. Illuminating the Invisible: Running high-fidelity simulations of turbulent flames and detonation to visualize the intricate processes that experiments can't capture. Finding the Signal in the Noise: Stripping massive datasets down to their governing physics and building fast, trustworthy surrogate models when answers are needed now. Bridging Disciplines: Thinking and communicating with engineers and scientists across the aisle to make complex combustion behavior understandable and usable.
